# Content transfer

For content people organize or reuse elsewhere, support appropriate drag sources and drop destinations with standard transferable types. Preview the items and distinguish valid destinations, insertion position, completion, and rejection. Batch multi-item drops as a coherent operation with recovery or undo, and keep a menu or button path for assistive input.

Use spring-loading when pausing over a container should reveal a deeper drop destination. Preserve source content on cancelled or failed transfers. Standard transferable representations also help cross-app and cross-device transfers; validate the actual formats instead of assuming any in-app model can be exported intact.

Sources: [Drag and drop](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/drag-and-drop).
