# Layout and navigation

## Fit content to the available space

Use the view or scene's available bounds and safe-area insets, including the keyboard, rather than a device model's dimensions. Keep essential controls reachable and unobscured; backgrounds and scrolling content can extend beneath system bars so Liquid Glass has content to reveal. Let content determine orientation support and density.

## Choose navigation by hierarchy

Use tabs for peer destinations and a navigation stack for drill-down within a destination. Keep tab identity and each tab's path stable; empty destinations explain their state rather than disappearing. Choose the number of tabs by frequency and available space, and avoid overflow where important destinations would become hidden. Labels belong with symbols.

Use system back navigation, including its edge gesture. Preserve selection, scroll position, filters, and drafts when switching destinations or returning from interruptions. Large titles suit roots with content to browse; choose title presentation for the actual screen rather than forcing every root to use one style.

Search belongs at the level of content it searches. Let system search placement adapt; a dedicated search tab is appropriate when searching is a major destination. On supported systems, tab-bar minimization and bottom accessories can keep ongoing content controls available while scrolling.
