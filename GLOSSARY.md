# Investment dashboard

Terms for investments held in a broker account and their performance over time.

## Language

**Trade**:
An executed purchase or sale of an investment. An order can produce multiple trades.
_Avoid_: Order, cash flow

**Cash flow**:
A movement of money into or out of a portfolio, such as a deposit or withdrawal.
_Avoid_: Trade

**Portfolio snapshot**:
A record of a portfolio's holdings and their valuations at a specified time.
_Avoid_: Trade history

**Price candle**:
An instrument's opening, highest, lowest, and closing prices over a specified interval, with trading volume when available.
_Avoid_: Tick
