# Unaru

Unaru is an app for personal use with Kite Connect.

## Language

**Kite account**:
The Zerodha trading account whose identity and data the user accesses through Unaru.
_Avoid_: Unaru account

**Kite session**:
The authorization granted by Kite for Unaru to access a Kite account until that
authorization expires or is invalidated.

**Connection pairing**:
Approval that permits a client to access an independently running backend. It is separate from signing in to a Kite account.

**Portfolio overview**:
A view of a Kite account's current holdings for long-term investment analysis,
with positions and trading funds as separate supporting information.

**Holding**:
A security or mutual fund investment currently owned in the Kite account.
Holdings are distinct from the account's trading positions.

**Invested cost**:
The acquisition cost attributed to investments currently held in the Kite account.
It excludes the cost of investments already sold.

**Current value**:
The value of current holdings at their available market prices or net asset values.

**Unrealised gain or loss**:
The difference between current value and invested cost for current holdings.
It excludes realised gains, received dividends, and annualised performance.

**Unrealised return percentage**:
Unrealised gain or loss expressed as a percentage of the invested cost of the
same holdings. A combined percentage uses their summed gains and costs.

**Allocation**:
A holding's share of the current value of holdings covered by the analysis.

**MTF holding**:
A holding purchased through Zerodha's Margin Trading Facility with broker financing.
